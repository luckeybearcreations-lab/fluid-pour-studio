Privacy Policy

Lucky Bear Fluid Studio ("the app") is made by Lucky Bear Creations. Last updated: October 20, 2026.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The app has no accounts and no analytics.

Ads in the Android app

The free version of the Android app shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. Pro removes it. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, rough location from your IP address, and how you interact with the ad. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time with the "Ad choices" link at the bottom of the studio. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Google → Ads. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your artwork

Pours, gallery saves and recipes are stored only on your device. When you export or share an image, it goes only where you choose to send it.

Purchases

In the Android app, Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card details. Google's privacy policy applies to that payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

On the website, Pro is bought through Stripe. Stripe handles the payment, and the site keeps a cookie that remembers your Pro purchase. Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
