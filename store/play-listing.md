**Full description**
Make fluid art without the mess. Lucky Bear Fluid Studio is a pocket paint studio where acrylic flows, blends and forms cells like it does on a real canvas.

• Pour with a cup you can see: flip cup, dirty pour, ribbon, tree ring, Dutch pour, swipe, bloom and more
• Tilt the canvas and watch the paint run to the edges
• Spin it on the turntable, then hit the brake
• Mix your own colors and paint types: fluid acrylic, heavy body, metallic, pearl, silicone mix
• Silicone cells that open up as the paint moves
• Undo and redo as much as you like
• Save recipes so you can pour your favorite combos again

Pouring is free, forever. Go Pro to save pours to your gallery and export print-ready images at up to 4× size.
